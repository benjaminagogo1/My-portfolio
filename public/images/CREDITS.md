# Portfolio image attribution

`benjamin-agogo-portrait.webp` is Otete Benjamin Agogo's portrait, supplied by the portfolio owner as `Me.jpg` on 18 November 2026. The full photograph was converted to WebP at its original 294 × 273 dimensions for the hero introduction, where CSS presents it in a circular frame.

`nurse-ng-preview.webp` is a genuine screenshot of the Nurse.ng public page, captured from its source code in an isolated local development environment on 17 November 2026 and converted to WebP.

The background photograph visible within the screenshot is **Learning to save lives**, by **Lindsay Mgbor / Department for International Development (DFID)**. It shows midwifery students and their instructor at the Jigawa State School of Midwifery, Nigeria.

- Original photograph: https://www.flickr.com/photos/dfid/7138904925/

The Nurse.ng source asset was resized and converted to WebP. Its application layout crops the photograph and adds gradient overlays. This portfolio captures that layout and compresses it to WebP. The photographic adaptation within the screenshot remains under CC BY-SA 2.0. No endorsement by the people pictured or the photographer is implied.

The HMSI-API diagram and social sharing image are original portfolio artwork made in HTML, SVG, CSS, and React.

`social-preview.png` and the project `*-social.png` files are generated from original portfolio HTML and CSS. The Nurse.ng and Agro-Shield project cards include crops of their existing public-page captures. The photograph within `nurse-ng-social.png` retains the Lindsay Mgbor / DFID attribution and CC BY-SA 2.0 terms described above. `hmsi-api-social.png` and `igede-language-si-social.png` use original explanatory diagrams.

`agro-shield-preview.webp` is a browser capture of Agro-Shield’s original public landing-page template, styling, branding, and marketing imagery from [the team repository](https://github.com/etoosammy01/Agro-Shield/tree/b12b4d3032a64656db0b33738bfb9b82f231cd13), served locally on 18 November 2026. It is not a capture of a verified public deployment. No authenticated pages, user-uploaded profile pictures, or member records were used. The original capture and source manifest are in `docs/verification/agro-shield-source.png` and `agro-shield-source.json`.
