# Project preview attribution

`nurse-ng-preview.webp` is a genuine screenshot of the Nurse.ng public page, captured from its source code in an isolated local development environment on 17 November 2026 and converted to WebP.

The background photograph visible within the screenshot is **Learning to save lives**, by **Lindsay Mgbor / Department for International Development (DFID)**. It shows midwifery students and their instructor at the Jigawa State School of Midwifery, Nigeria.

- Original photograph: https://www.flickr.com/photos/dfid/7138904925/
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Learning_to_save_lives_(7138904925).jpg
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 Generic](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/)

The Nurse.ng source asset was resized and converted to WebP. Its application layout crops the photograph and adds gradient overlays. This portfolio captures that layout and compresses it to WebP. The photographic adaptation within the screenshot remains under CC BY-SA 2.0. No endorsement by the people pictured or the photographer is implied.

The HMSI-API diagram and social sharing image are original portfolio artwork made in HTML, SVG, CSS, and React.
